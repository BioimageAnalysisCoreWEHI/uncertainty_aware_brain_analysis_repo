Before starting, write down what counts as a failure. Suggested failure modes: (1) the atlas outline does not sit on the tissue; (2) extreme warping or very wobbly boundaries; (3) under-deformed — the outline fits but internal boundaries are offset; (4) other. Rate each registration as pass, fail or unsure. With "half from borderline / likely failures" (recommended), half the sample comes from registrations that fail the starting point or sit near it (lowest IoU / highest grad_p95 quartile of the passing ones), so that enough failures are seen to place a threshold; the metrics only choose the sample and are never shown. Two raters should use the same seed and settings so that they rate the same registrations. Progress is kept in this browser; export the CSV when you finish.
